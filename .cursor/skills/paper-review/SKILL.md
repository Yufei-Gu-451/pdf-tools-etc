# Paper Review

## Procedure

1. Read the full paper, including appendices and supplements.
2. Identify the problem, main claim, method, theory, experiments, and conclusions.
3. Verify every critique against theorem assumptions, metrics, protocols, figures, and appendices.
4. Present concise bullet points under:
   - Main summary
   - Strengths
   - Weaknesses
   - Discussion questions
   - Overall assessment, only when requested

## Review principles

- Prioritize structural limitations and causal validity over minor technical objections.
- Distinguish internal validity from generalization, correlation from causation, and theoretical algorithms from practical implementations.
- Check theory–practice gaps, initialization dependence, schedule confounders, baseline and tuning fairness, uncertainty, effect size, compute or throughput claims, metric choice, and whether experiments isolate the proposed mechanism.
- Ensure comparisons use matched algorithms, schedules, budgets, measurements, and evaluation periods; qualify conclusions when they do not.
- Treat restrictive assumptions as limits on scope, not proof that a result is invalid.
- Prefer precise wording such as “does not establish,” “does not isolate,” and “does not rule out.”

## Refining a draft

- Preserve the user’s structure, context, and intended criticism.
- Make only minor wording and grammar revisions unless broader rewriting is approved.
- Correct factual errors and briefly qualify claims that overreach the evidence.
- Add only a few high-value missing perspectives; do not create extra sections unless requested.
- Keep the response concise and use bullet points.
